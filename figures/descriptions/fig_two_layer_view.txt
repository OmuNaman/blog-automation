Two-Layer View: Receptive Field Expands to 5 Tokens

IMPORTANT: Pure white background (#FFFFFF). No gray tint, no colored background, no gradient. The diagram must have a clean white background suitable for an academic paper/blog post.

This diagram shows how stacking two SWA layers expands the receptive field for token "mat". Layout is three horizontal rows (layers) stacked vertically.

TOP ROW - "Layer 2":
- A single highlighted token box at position 7, labeled "mat", with pastel purple fill. This is the focus token.

MIDDLE ROW - "Layer 1":
- 8 token boxes in a row.
- Positions 5, 6, 7 ("warm", "soft", "mat") are highlighted with pastel blue fill (directly attended by "mat" at layer 2).
- Positions 0-4 are light gray (not directly accessible at this step).
- Three solid black arrows from "mat" (layer 2) down to positions 5, 6, 7 (layer 1).

BOTTOM ROW - "Layer 0 (Input)":
- 8 token boxes in a row.
- Positions 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 ("on", "the", "warm", "soft", "mat") are highlighted with pastel green fill. These are the positions accessible because:
  - "warm" (5) at layer 1 attended to positions 3, 4, 5
  - "soft" (6) at layer 1 attended to positions 4, 5, 6
  - "mat" (7) at layer 1 attended to positions 5, 6, 7
- Positions 0, 1, 2 are light gray (still outside the expanded receptive field).
- Arrows from each layer 1 token down to its respective window at layer 0:
  - From "warm" (5) at layer 1 to positions 3, 4, 5 at layer 0 (thin gray arrows)
  - From "soft" (6) at layer 1 to positions 4, 5, 6 at layer 0 (thin gray arrows)
  - From "mat" (7) at layer 1 to positions 5, 6, 7 at layer 0 (thin gray arrows)

WINDOW BRACKETS:
- At layer 1: bracket under positions 5-7 labeled "Direct window (w=3)"
- At layer 0: bracket under positions 3-7 labeled "Expanded receptive field (2w-1=5)"

ANNOTATION:

- "Indirect access through relay tokens at layer 1"
