Layer Contribution Fractions in a 6-Layer Standard Transformer

IMPORTANT: Pure white background (#FFFFFF). No gray tint, no gradient.

== CONTEXT ==
This diagram shows how each layer's contribution becomes a progressively smaller fraction of the total hidden state in a standard residual transformer. With 6 layers plus the embedding, each contributes roughly 1/7 of the final hidden state, meaning important early-layer features are "drowned out."

== LAYOUT ==
- Two parts side by side
- Left: A stacked bar showing the hidden state composition at layer 6
- Right: A visual showing the "drowning" effect with annotations
