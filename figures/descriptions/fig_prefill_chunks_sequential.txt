Chunked Pre-fill in Action: Processing an 8-Token Prompt

This diagram shows the chunked pre-fill process step by step for our 8-token sequence with w=3. Layout is top-to-bottom with three processing stages.

TOP - "The Full Prompt":
- A horizontal row of 8 token boxes: "The", "cat", "sat", "on", "the", "warm", "soft", "mat".
- Dashed vertical lines divide the sequence into 3 chunks:
  - Chunk 1: tokens 0-2 ("The", "cat", "sat") with a light blue bracket
  - Chunk 2: tokens 3-5 ("on", "the", "warm") with a light green bracket
  - Chunk 3: tokens 6-7 ("soft", "mat") with a light orange bracket

STAGE 1 - "Process Chunk 1":
- Show tokens "The", "cat", "sat" highlighted in pastel blue.
- A small 3x3 attention matrix next to them (lower triangle filled, blue).
- An arrow to a small buffer showing: [K_0/V_0, K_1/V_1, K_2/V_2] in pastel blue.
- Label: "Compute attention within chunk, fill KV cache slots 0-2"

STAGE 2 - "Process Chunk 2":

- A small 3x3 attention matrix (green).
- The buffer now shows: [K_3/V_3, K_4/V_4, K_5/V_5] in pastel green (overwritten because rolling buffer with w=3).

- Show tokens "soft", "mat" highlighted in pastel orange.

BOTTOM:
- "Pre-fill complete. Rolling buffer contains the most recent w entries. Ready for autoregressive generation."
